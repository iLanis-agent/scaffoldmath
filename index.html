The boundary

Scaffold Math keeps reach, tower and plank arithmetic only. It gives no verdicts about load ratings or safe assembly - what holds weight is the manufacturer's rating, and the manual owns guardrails, outriggers and ties. Never improvise a stack, and follow local height-safety rules.

The box promises a twelve-foot reach. The reach includes you. Scaffold Math does the honest arithmetic: how high the platform really stands, how many towers the wall actually takes, and how many planks the levels drink.

Three panels. The reach truth subtracts the person norm from the claimed reach. The tower truth divides your wall by what one tower covers. The plank truth multiplies planks per level by the levels you plan to work.
